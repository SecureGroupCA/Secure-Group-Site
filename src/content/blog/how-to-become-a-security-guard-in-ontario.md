Security guard work is one of the fastest ways to get a steady job in the GTA. The licence takes a few weeks, the course is affordable, and companies like ours are always hiring.

Here is the whole path, step by step.

## Who can apply

You need to:

- Be **18 or older**
- Be **legally allowed to work in Canada**
- Have a **clean criminal record** (some minor records may be okay; serious ones are not)
- Be able to communicate in English

## Step 1: Take the 40-hour course

The Ontario government requires a **40-hour training course** from an approved provider. It covers the law, how to write reports, how to handle emergencies, and how to deal with people calmly.

Many providers offer it online. Cost is usually **$150 to $300**. Pick one that is listed as approved by the Ministry of the Solicitor General.

You will also need a valid **Emergency First Aid and CPR** certificate. If you do not have one, many course providers bundle it in.

## Step 2: Pass the provincial test

After the course, you book the **Ontario security guard test**. It is multiple choice and costs about **$35**. You get your result the same day.

Study the course material. Most people pass on the first try if they read everything.

## Step 3: Apply for your licence

Apply online through ServiceOntario. You will need:

- Your course completion number
- Your test result
- Two pieces of ID
- A passport-style photo
- The licence fee (around **$80**)

The province runs a criminal background check. Your licence usually arrives within a couple of weeks.

## Step 4: Get hired

Now the fun part. Here is how to stand out:

- **Apply directly to local companies**, not just job boards. Companies in Vaughan, Brampton and Mississauga always need guards for warehouses and construction sites.
- **Say what shifts you can work.** Nights and weekends get you hired fastest.
- **Mention your transport.** Many sites are not near transit. A car or a reliable ride is a big plus.
- **Be reachable.** Companies call when a shift opens. Answer your phone.

## What the job is really like

Most new guards start on a gate, a lobby or a patrol. You will write reports, check doors, greet people and watch for problems. It is quiet most of the time, and you need to stay sharp when it is not.

Good guards move up fast. Supervisors, site leads and operations managers almost all started on a post.
